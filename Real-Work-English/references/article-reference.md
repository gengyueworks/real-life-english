# Article Reference｜和代理确认新品上市方案

## 这份样章展示了什么

这份样章展示了如何把一场真实工作会议转化成可以反复练习的英语资料。它围绕区域IMC与代理销售负责人讨论新品上市方案的场景展开。

第一部分交代真实业务背景，让学习者先理解会议对象、业务问题和岗位权限。第二部分把零散会议信息整理成一场可以直接跟读的完整对话，重点训练观点表达、自然承接和商务讨论。第三部分提取会议中最有压力的冲突，让学习者先自己回应，再查看参考表达。第四部分要求学习者独立讲清一个与本章经历直接相关的业务问题，训练结构化表达能力。

# 第三章｜新品上市前确认营销策略、渠道资源和时间

## 1. 真实背景

### 【真实业务事实】会议背景

Market A准备推广Product A和Product B两款新品。代理判断近期销售趋势不理想，竞品正在通过电视广告、促销价格和其他营销动作争夺市场，因此希望尽快启动推广，不想等到节日高峰期再行动。

代理计划使用电视广告、户外广告、广播、渠道联合营销和数字广告。总部团队可以负责paid search和paid social，但正式启动前仍需确认促销周期、重点产品、本地内容、零售渠道承接和预算方向。

此前的项目复盘显示，Market A本地制作的local-language content表现更好。代理正在制作本地UGC，但部分素材还没有完成。其他区域团队制作的素材由各区域自行投入，不能默认免费提供给Market A。

> 本章主对话属于【整理英文】。开场small talk和部分客户回应属于【模拟训练】，不代表客户真实原话、承诺或合作结果。

## 2. 可以直接跟读的完整英文对话

### 【整理英文与模拟训练】新品上市协调会议

**Regional IMC**

Hi. Good to see you. I saw the outdoor display photo you shared.

**Distributor Sales VP**

Yes, it is near a shopping district. This season is busy there, so we want to build more visibility.

**Regional IMC**

It looks very visible. We have also been reviewing the launch materials, so the photo helped me connect the online and offline work.

**Distributor Sales VP**

Good. We want the activities to work together.

**Regional IMC**

Great. Shall we get started?

Today, I would like to align on the launch timing and the conditions we still need to confirm. By the end, we should have a clear direction and a follow-up date.

**Distributor Sales VP**

My main concern is timing. Sales have not been good, and competitors are moving aggressively. We want to start the TV campaign soon and have HQ manage paid search and paid social.

**Regional IMC**

So your priority is to build reach quickly, then use digital ads to explain the products. I agree that we need to move fast.

For the paid campaign, we need one confirmed price period. What is the first promotion window you can commit to?

**Distributor Sales VP**

We want the new price to start with the TV campaign and continue through the holiday period. If competitors make another price move, we may need to adjust again.

**Regional IMC**

I understand. You can confirm the first price, while the holiday offer may still change. I suggest two phases. We start with the first offer, then review competitor actions before the holiday peak. This keeps the first message stable and gives us room to adjust.

**Distributor Sales VP**

I still prefer to go live immediately. Waiting for every detail may make us too slow.

**Regional IMC**

We do not need every detail. We only need the basic launch conditions. The first offer and hero products must be confirmed. The product pages and creatives also need to be live.

If these items are ready, we can set the go-live date while the holiday plan continues in parallel.

**Distributor Sales VP**

That is acceptable. The hero products will be Product A and Product B. I still need to confirm the price period and stock with the HQ Sales Lead.

**Regional IMC**

Good. Let us keep the go-live date open until that confirmation. I will prepare the campaign around Product A and Product B in parallel.

The next issue is local content. In the previous campaign, original local-language content performed better than many adapted assets. What local UGC can be ready for phase one?

**Distributor Sales VP**

Some local videos are in production, but they may not all be ready before the first campaign date. We would like HQ to provide more UGC from other markets so that we can start faster.

**Regional IMC**

Existing assets can help us start faster. For phase one, HQ can coordinate suitable assets. For phase two, the local team can produce a few local-language hero videos. This gives us speed now and stronger local content later. Would this split work for you?

**Distributor Sales VP**

We are already spending heavily on TV and other local media. It will be difficult to add another content budget now.

**Regional IMC**

I understand the budget pressure. Broad exposure still needs product content that explains why customers should choose Product A or Product B.

Could we start with a few hero videos and expand after we review the results?

**Distributor Sales VP**

That is more realistic. I cannot promise the budget yet, but I can ask for a smaller production option.

**Regional IMC**

That works. HQ will confirm the key selling points, and the local team can adapt the content. Please ask your marketing contact to confirm the video scope and delivery date by email.

**Distributor Sales VP**

The agency will manage the content, and I will follow the key items with our marketing contact.

**Regional IMC**

Good. We still need a direction for the paid media budget. Can your team confirm a working budget range today?

**Distributor Sales VP**

I cannot confirm it today. Most of the next budget may go to TV. I would prefer HQ to send the plan first.

**Regional IMC**

It is reasonable to see the campaign direction first. We can prepare the structure, but the allocation still needs a working budget range. A small range and a larger range require different priorities. Without it, we cannot decide the campaign period or spending focus. HQ support is also pending internal approval.

Could you give us a rough range by Monday?

**Distributor Sales VP**

I can give you a direction by Monday, but the final number may still need approval. What if the range changes after we see the plan?

**Regional IMC**

That is fine. We will use it as a working range, not as final approval. If the range changes, we can adjust the media allocation before launch.

Okay. I’ll send the follow-up details by email after the meeting.

### 模拟一｜价格还没确认，但代理要求广告立即上线

**Distributor Sales VP**

We cannot wait for the final price. Please start the paid campaign now, and we will update the offer later.

先暂停，自己回答。你的回应需要承认市场紧迫性，同时说明广告启动所需的最低条件。

这轮练习的沟通动作是先确认对方对速度的要求，再区分最低启动条件和可以后补的事项，最后锁定确认时间。

**Regional IMC**

Moving quickly makes sense, especially while competitors are active. We can start before every holiday detail is final, but phase one still needs a confirmed offer and available stock. The product pages and first creatives also need to be live. Otherwise, customers may see the wrong offer or enter pages that are not ready. If your team confirms these launch conditions, we can set the go-live date. Can you confirm them by Monday?

#### 本轮可复用表达

1. `We can start before..., but... still needs...`。用于先接受部分要求，再说明最低条件。例句 `We can start before every holiday detail is final, but phase one still needs a confirmed offer.`
2. `Otherwise, ... may...`。用于解释条件不足可能带来的影响。例句 `Otherwise, customers may see the wrong offer.`
3. `If..., we can...`。用于提出启动条件和对应的下一步。例句 `If your team confirms the launch conditions, we can set the go-live date.`

**Distributor Sales VP**

HQ already has many UGC videos from other markets. Can we use all of them instead of spending more on local production?

这轮练习的沟通动作是承认使用现有素材的效率价值，说明本地效果和资源边界，再提出分阶段内容方案。

<summary>展开参考回应</summary>

**Regional IMC**

Using existing UGC can save time and cost. We can check which assets are available, but other regions funded their own production. The previous campaign also showed that original local-language content performed better. If we rely only on adapted videos, the local response may be weaker. For phase one, we can use suitable existing assets. For phase two, the local team can produce a few local-language hero videos, while HQ confirms the key selling points. #### 本轮可复用表达

1. `For phase one... For phase two...`。用于把短期方案和后续方案分开表达。例句 `For phase one, we can use suitable existing assets. For phase two, the local team can produce local-language hero videos.`
2. `If we rely only on..., ... may...`。用于说明单一方案可能造成的风险。例句 `If we rely only on adapted videos, the local response may be weaker.`
3. `..., while...`。用于划分双方同时推进的职责。例句 `The local team can produce the videos, while HQ confirms the key selling points.`

We need to see your full media plan before we decide how much budget to put into digital.

<summary>展开参考回应</summary>

**Regional IMC**

## 4. Opinion Presentation Practice

### 【模拟训练】观点表达问题

**How would you set objectives for different marketing channels during a major sales campaign?**

### 业务背景

假设你正在为Market A策划一次重要大促。当地高客单价产品主要在线下成交，但消费者会通过广告、社交媒体和产品页面了解品牌。代理还会投入电视广告、户外广告和本地渠道资源。你需要说明怎样让各个渠道承担不同任务，并共同服务最终sell-out。

### 思考路径

先说明这次大促的总体业务目标，例如推动新品、清理库存或提升sell-out。接着按照消费者购买旅程，为不同营销渠道分配任务，并设置与任务对应的指标。然后检查价格、库存和渠道承接是否支持这些目标。最后说明你会怎样根据实际结果调整内容、预算或零售支持。

### 可以使用的关键词

- `campaign objective`
- `channel role`
- `customer journey`
- `sell-out`
- `channel readiness`

先独立表达约90秒，再展开参考表达。

<details>
<summary>展开参考表达</summary>

### 参考表达

In my view, I would start with one business objective. For example, the campaign may need to launch hero products or improve sell-out during a holiday period. Each channel should support a different part of that goal.

TV and outdoor media can build broad awareness. I would look at reach and brand search. Paid social can explain the product value and create interest. I would review video response and product page visits.

The website and social pages should help customers compare products and trust the brand. Retail stores then support the final sale. I would look at product availability and sell-out in the priority channels.

From the business side, these objectives only work when the offer and stock are ready. The key stores also need the right products. Strong awareness has limited value if customers cannot find the product.

During the campaign, I would review each channel against its role. If awareness grows but sell-out stays weak, I would locate where the customer journey breaks. Then I would adjust the content or retail support. Any major budget change would still need internal approval.

### 本轮可复用表达

1. `I would start with one overall business objective.`。用于先给出整套回答的起点。例句 `I would start with one overall business objective for the holiday campaign.`
2. `First..., then..., after that...`。用于按顺序说明自己的工作方法。例句 `First, I would define the campaign objective. Then, I would set the role of each channel. After that, I would review the results.`
3. `I would look at three factors, X, Y and Z.`。用于列出最重要的判断维度。例句 `I would look at three factors, the campaign objective, the channel role and the business result.`
4. `If X is growing but Y stays weak, I would locate where...`。用于表达发现结果不一致时的诊断思路。例句 `If awareness is growing but sell-out stays weak, I would locate where the customer journey breaks.`

</details>
