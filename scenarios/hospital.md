# 就医 Doctor & Hospital

> 这个场景你会在哪用到：挂号、跟医生描述症状、拿处方、买药、报销——在异国他乡看病时的沟通。

## 常用词

| 中文 | 英文 | 场景例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 挂号 / 预约 | **make an appointment** | "I'd like to make an appointment."（我想预约挂号。） |
| 症状 | **symptom** | "What are your symptoms?"（你有什么症状？） |
| 发烧 | **fever** | "I've had a fever since last night."（我昨晚开始发烧。） |
| 咳嗽 | **cough** | "I can't stop coughing."（我一直咳不停。） |
| 头疼 | **headache** | "I have a pounding headache."（我头疼得厉害。） |
| 处方 | **prescription** | "Where do I fill this prescription?"（这处方去哪拿药？） |
| 药房 | **pharmacy** | "Is there a pharmacy nearby?"（附近有药房吗？） |
| 过敏 | **allergic** | "I'm allergic to penicillin."（我对青霉素过敏。） |
| 保险 | **insurance** | "Do you take my insurance?"（你们收我的保险吗？） |
| 急诊 | **emergency room / ER** | "I need to go to the emergency room."（我得去急诊。） |
| 量体温 | **take temperature** | "Let me take your temperature."（我给你量个体温。） |
| 抽血 | **blood test** | "You'll need a blood test."（你需要抽个血。） |
| 拍片 / X 光 | **X-ray** | "The doctor ordered an X-ray."（医生开了 X 光。） |
| 复诊 | **follow-up** | "When should I come back for a follow-up?"（我什么时候回来复诊？） |
| 报销 | **reimbursement** | "Can I get a receipt for reimbursement?"（能给我报销用的收据吗？） |
| 挂号费 | **registration fee** | "How much is the registration fee?"（挂号费多少？） |

## 常用句

- "It hurts when I breathe."（我一呼吸就疼。）
- "I've been feeling sick for three days."（我难受三天了。）
- "Is it serious?"（情况严重吗？）
- "How often should I take this?"（这药多久吃一次？）
